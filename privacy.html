Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Tally app ("Tally") collects, why, and what rights you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Who is responsible

David Max Urban, Czechia (the "operator"). Contact: tally0application@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (the password is stored in a protected form, we cannot read it).
  • Profile: display name and birth date (the birth date is used only to check age limits and is never shown to other people). Optional: avatar choices, profile photo, up to 3 titles.
  • Club use: clubs, groups and teams you belong to, attendance records, event replies, loyalty points and streaks, announcements you read or post.
  • Children: a parent or legal guardian can add a child to a club (first name, optional last name, birth year, optional phone number for the coach). The child does not have an account.
  • Messages: club chat and friend messages, friend requests, blocks and reports you make.
  • Feedback: messages you send through "Found a bug? Got an idea?", with your account, phone type (Android or iOS) and app language.
  • Agreements: a record of when you accepted the terms, or when a parent confirmed consent for a child.

We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising ID, and we do not use advertising or analytics services. The camera is used only to scan a friend's QR code on your device. Nothing is recorded or saved. Photos are used only when you pick one yourself.

Why we use it

To run the app: sign you in, show your clubs, take attendance, deliver messages, and keep people safe (age checks, reporting, blocking, removing abusive accounts). Our legal basis is performing the service you asked for, the consent you give (for children, the parent's consent), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe.

Children and ages

Chat, friends and groups are only for people aged 15 and over, checked from the birth date, and enforced by our servers. Younger children appear only under a parent's or guardian's account. When a parent adds a child, the parent confirms that they are the parent or legal guardian and consents to the storage of the child's data as described here. Profile photos of members can be switched off by the club.

Who can see what

  • Club staff see attendance and profiles of their club's members.
  • Members of the same club or group see your display name, avatar, titles and (if allowed by the club) your profile photo.
  • Friends see your display name and avatar.
  • Nobody sees your birth date or email. If you leave "Friends can find me by email" switched on, a person who already knows your exact email can send you a friend request. They are never told whether an email has an account.

Where data is stored and who processes it

Data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). App updates and builds are delivered through Expo (EAS), which receives technical information needed to deliver an update (such as app version and platform). We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, avatar, titles, photos, friends, messages and memberships are deleted. Attendance entries kept by a club may remain with that club without your name. Reports about abuse and records of consent may be kept for up to 12 months for safety and legal reasons. Backups may hold deleted data for a short period before they expire.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a portable copy, and you can object to processing. You can delete your account any time in the app (Settings, Delete my account) or by following the steps on the account deletion page. For anything else, write to tally0application@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (uoou.gov.cz), or to the authority in your own country.

Safety and reports

If you report a message, a copy of it and the reason are stored for review. Accounts that break the terms can be suspended, and their messages removed.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
