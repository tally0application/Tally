Delete your Tally account

You can delete your Tally account and its data in two ways.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tally and tap your picture (top right), then Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type the confirmation word and tap Delete my account forever.

Option 2: by email, without the app

Write to tally0application@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Tally account", from the email address you registered with. We confirm by reply and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, display name, avatar, titles and profile photo
  • Your friends, friend requests and messages
  • Your club and group memberships
  • Clubs where you are the only member

What may remain

  • Attendance entries kept by a club may stay with that club without your name. If you added a child, ask the club owner or write to us to have the child's data removed too.
  • Reports about abuse and records of consent, for up to 12 months, for safety and legal reasons.
  • Backups, for a short period until they expire.

If you own a club

A club that still has other members must be handed over or emptied first. A club where you are the only member is deleted together with your account.
