Tally

The friendly attendance app for sports clubs and training groups.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store
For coachesAdd athletes, plan weekly trainings and one-time events, mark who showed up, and post announcements.
For parentsAdd your child, sign them in for trainings and watch their progress grow. Sickness never breaks a streak.
For athletes and friendsEarn points and unlock avatar items, join casual groups, and message friends. Chat and friends are for ages 15 and over.
